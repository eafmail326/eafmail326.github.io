Privacy Policy

This tool is operated by EAF BioSolutions LLC for its own internal use. It connects to the operator's own Google account to copy the operator's own Google Drive files to the operator's own cloud storage.

It does not collect, sell, share or transfer data to any third party. Files are copied directly from the operator's Google Drive to the operator's own pCloud storage, using software running on the operator's own computer. No data is stored on any other server.
